MasterLine (by Lupitul) – Delete your data

MasterLine has no accounts of its own. Your data is kept in two places, and you can delete both.

1. Progress on your phone

Coins, diamonds, items, levels, settings and statistics are saved on your device. To delete them, uninstall MasterLine (or go to Android Settings → Apps → MasterLine → Storage → Clear storage).

2. Progress saved to your Google account

If you signed in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress, your achievements and your leaderboard scores are stored by Google. To delete them:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app (or Play Store → your profile → Play Games).
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, find MasterLine and tap Delete.

Ask us to delete it

You can also email the developer at the address shown in the privacy policy with the subject “MasterLine data deletion”. We will help you delete everything within 30 days.

What is deleted and kept

Deleting removes all game progress, items, achievements and scores. Records of in-app purchases are kept by Google Play for its legal and accounting needs; we do not keep any data ourselves.
