MasterLine – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how the MasterLine game ("the app"), published on Google Play by Lupitul, handles information.

Game progress

Your game progress (coins, diamonds, items, levels, settings and statistics) is saved on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of this progress is also saved to your Google account using Google Play Games "Saved Games", so you can restore it on another phone. This copy is stored by Google, not on our own servers.

Google Play Games

When you sign in to Google Play Games, the app uses your Play Games player profile (player ID and display name) to show who is signed in, save your progress, unlock achievements and submit leaderboard scores. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and your Play Games settings. Signing in is optional; the game works without it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. Google Play tells the app which item was bought so the game can deliver it.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service we use to find and fix bugs. A report contains the error details, the app version, your device model and Android version, and a random ID for this installation. It does not contain your name, email or contacts, and it is not used for advertising. Google processes these reports for us under the Firebase privacy terms.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not show ads, does not use advertising identifiers and does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell any data.

Random-item chests

Some chests contain random items. The chance of getting each rarity is shown in the game before you open or buy a chest.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

To delete progress stored on your device, uninstall the app. To delete progress saved to your Google account, open Google Play Games settings and delete the MasterLine data, or email us and we will help.
